ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology for Safer Careers

FakeJobDetector is a student project designed to help people identify possible fake job and internship offers. It uses an AI model and supporting checks to highlight potential warning signs in job descriptions.

Our Mission: Help students and job seekers recognize possible recruitment scams and make informed decisions.

STAY ALERT

Safety Tips for Job Seekers

A few important checks can help you stay safer.

💰

Be Careful with Fees

Be cautious when an employer asks for registration or interview fees.

🏢

Verify the Employer

Check the official company website and careers page.

🔐

Protect Personal Data

Never share passwords, OTPs, or sensitive information with unknown contacts.

⚠️

Question Unrealistic Offers

Review unusually high salaries, urgent requests, and guaranteed job promises carefully.
