About FakeJobDetector

FakeJobDetector is a student project that helps people identify possible fake job and internship offers. It uses an AI model to analyse job descriptions and highlights warning signs such as registration fees and unrealistic promises.

Our Mission: To help students and job seekers recognise possible recruitment scams and make safer decisions.

🛡️ Safety Tips for Job Seekers

💰 Avoid Payment Requests

Be careful if an employer asks you to pay registration or interview fees.

🏢 Verify the Company

Check the company's official website and careers page before applying.

🔐 Protect Your Details

Do not share passwords, OTPs, or sensitive information with unknown people.

⚠️ Check Job Offers

Be cautious of unrealistic salaries, urgent requests, and guaranteed job promises.
